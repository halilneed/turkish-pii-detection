# Roadmap

## Current release: improve usability

- Keep model, companion repository, and article links consistent.
- Execute the inference example on a recorded environment and save a fictional reproduction.
- Recover and publish permitted original training configuration, generator, label dictionary, and evaluation artifacts with their licenses.

## Next masking candidate

Focus evaluation on multiple people, Turkish suffixes, long inputs, negative examples, and instruction scope. Compare the existing release, an identified baseline, and the candidate on the same frozen policy. Report improvements alongside regressions. No candidate weights, completion date, or improved score are announced yet.

## Separate research idea: Turkish data classification

A future classifier would return data-category labels and evidence for review, rather than rewritten text. It needs its own label policy, data, baseline, and evaluation. Classification is not a legal compliance decision. Corporate confidentiality labels also require an explicit organizational policy.
